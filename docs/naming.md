# Naming sprint (2026-09-27)

Method: the Wolff Olins naming handbook (brief, generate, filter, present). Domain checks run live against the .fun (Radix), .xyz (CentralNic) and .com (Verisign) registries on 2026-09-27. Availability changes daily; re-check before buying.

## Brief

- **Offer:** a zk memecoin launchpad on Linea. Sealed launch windows (hidden orders, one clearing price), a public bonding curve, graduation to Uniswap v4 with liquidity locked forever, and half the protocol fee burns ETH.
- **Who it's for:** memecoin creators and traders, worldwide (large Asian share), crypto-native, tired of snipers and rugs. Secondary: the Linea ecosystem.
- **Distinctive:** fairness you can verify. "Prove everything. Reveal nothing."
- **Personality:** playful, cheeky, retro-desktop, but trustworthy. Memes with integrity.
- **Should make people feel:** it's fun and it's fair. **Do:** launch and trade.
- **Parent relationship:** built *on* Linea, not *by* Linea. No "Linea" in the name.
- **Criteria:** meaning (mint, seal, proof, fairness), strategic fit (survives new chains and products), sound (short, fixed stress, no "th", easy in Mandarin, Japanese, Spanish), availability (.fun free, no major crypto brand clash), bonus if it works as a verb.

## Filter

`.fun` free on 2026-09-27: foundr, mintmark, clink, sealpad, memeproof, sealmint, mold, smelt, unseal, mintage, reeded, obverse, sprue, minto, sealo, zeel, waxseal, mintwax, sealwax, plonk, zkmint, sigil, minty, cachet, stamped, emboss, plopp, sealbox, hushmint, mintseal.

`.com` was taken for every candidate except foundreth (checked earlier).

Cut for meaning or sound: mold (fungus), clink (UK slang for jail), plonk (cheap wine), sprue and planchet (too obscure), obverse and cachet (hard to say across languages), reeded, emboss, stamped (flat), minto and zeel (existing consumer brands).

Crypto brand collisions (web search): **Sigil** (several live tokens: Sigil Finance, SIGIL Protocol, The Sigil on Solana), **Minty** (NFT app, Shopify app, crypto stream). **Mintmark**, **Sealpad**, **Hushmint**: no crypto brand found.

## Shortlist

| Name | Type | .fun | Why | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mintmark** | Evocative | free | A mint mark is the small letter on a coin that proves which mint struck it: provenance you can verify. Maps straight onto "prove everything". Coin world, fits the stamped-coin visuals. Universal sounds (m, n, t, k), alliteration, fixed stress. | 8 letters. A real numismatic term, so trademark strength needs a lawyer's view. |
| **foundr** (control) | Evocative | free | founder + foundry double meaning, short, already designed around. | Foundr (startup media brand, foundr.com) and Foundry (the Ethereum dev toolkit: forge, cast, anvil). |
| **Hushmint** | Descriptive-evocative | free | Says the privacy USP out loud; memey ("hush money"). | "Hush money" also reads as bribery. Privacy + money is a regulatory optics risk. Spanish lacks "sh". |
| **Sealpad** | Descriptive | free | Says exactly what's different: sealed launches. Clear to traders. | Literal, so less memorable; "-pad" is generic launchpad-speak. |
| **Minty** | Coined | free | Friendly, tiny, fresh launches, easy in every language. | Crowded: several NFT and crypto products called Minty. |

## Recommendation

**Mintmark**, with the descriptor "the zk memecoin launchpad on Linea" until the name carries itself. Keep **foundrETH** out of the brand; if the brand changes, the burn counter becomes the "mintmark burn" or similar.

Next steps before committing: trademark search in classes 9, 36 and 42 by a lawyer; a native-speaker check in Mandarin, Japanese, Korean and Spanish; register mintmark.fun and foundr.fun defensively (both free today).

## Second pass (2026-09-27, after the Dragons' Den tournament)

145 candidates in five families (compounds, numismatic terms, coined words, privacy words, .fun phrases, meme names). Every .fun checked live (whois, then RDAP at rdap.radix.host for rate-limited lookups; finalists re-checked by whois for reserved status). 128 were free.

Cut for crypto collisions: Doubloon (DBL token), Ducat (four projects), Blockzero (Blockzero Labs launchpad), Nummo (Swiss finance app), Mintery (trademarked crypto wallet), Squaremint (Square/Block payments). BlindMint is clear as a brand, but "blind mint" is an existing NFT term for not knowing what you get.
